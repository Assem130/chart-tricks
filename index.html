Three different changes

Zoom: change the range on the axis. A triangle marks a value outside it. The value is clipped, not deleted.

Hide: remove a flagged marker from view. Its value still contributes to every statistic.

Calculate: mean adds all values and divides by the count. Median is the middle sorted value, or the average of the two middle values.

Outliers fall strictly below Q1 − 1.5 × IQR or above Q3 + 1.5 × IQR. Q1 and Q3 are medians of the lower and upper halves. For odd counts, exclude the middle value from both halves.

All scales are linear. Only monthly revenue points are connected. Orders and survey groups have no implied time trend.
