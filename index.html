How the statistics work

Mean adds all values and divides by the count. Median is the middle sorted value, or the average of the two middle values.

Outliers fall strictly below Q1 − 1.5 × IQR or above Q3 + 1.5 × IQR. Q1 and Q3 are medians of the lower and upper halves. For odd counts, the middle value is excluded from both halves.

Hiding a point changes only its visibility. Bounds can clip points. Neither operation changes the data, quartiles, mean or median.

All scales are linear. Only monthly revenue points are connected; orders and survey groups have no implied time trend.
